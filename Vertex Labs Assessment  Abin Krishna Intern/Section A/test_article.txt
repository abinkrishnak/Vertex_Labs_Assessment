Southeast Asia deal roundup: six startups, one busy week

Hanoi-based edtech Lumio Learn raised S$15 million (about US$11.1 million) in a Series B co-led by Orchid Capital and Meridian Partners, with Sakura Ventures participating.

Manila digital health startup Bayanihan Health closed a ₱500 million Series A led by Tagus Growth. Separately, Cambridge-based battery maker VoltaGrid secured $8 million in a seed extension, though it did not name a lead.

Data-infrastructure firm Stratus Mesh, valued at $1.2 billion after the deal, raised a Series D from Apex Crossover Fund and others but did not say how much. Agritech startups Paddy Pulse and Tidal Crop announced a joint $6 million seed round led by Delta Seed Fund.

Rival Quanta Farm, which raised a $20 million Series A last year, was acquired by GreenRoot this week for an undisclosed sum.
